# POV: when you prank your teacher (blocky 3D, v3)

Meme format: fixed POV title, no narration, absurd escalation (teacher turns into a cow, banana phone,
a cop on a horse through the wall, rocket to the moon). ~28 s. Silent on purpose: add a trending sound in TikTok.

- `assets/world.js` — classroom (posters, shelf, lockers, windows, halloween corner), courtroom, moon,
  avatars, slime/poof/brick/rocket effects and every shot (beats in `build.py`).
- Meme faces (`drawMeme` in `world.js`): rage (wrinkles, veins, gritted teeth), shock, laugh-cry, cry,
  cool (sunglasses) and dead/bruh.
- `build.py` — beat times, overlay (title, banana phone call, stamp, speech bubble, end card).

Render: `npx hyperframes render -o out.mp4`
