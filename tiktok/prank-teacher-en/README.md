# POV: when you prank your teacher (blocky 3D)

Meme format: fixed POV title, no narration, sound effects only (add a trending sound in TikTok). ~25 s.
Own animal avatars (cat student, bear teacher), slime instead of violence, courtroom twist.

- `assets/world.js` — classroom + courtroom, avatars, slime/steam effects and every shot (beats in `build.py`).
- `build.py` — beat times, overlay (title, phone call, stamp, speech bubble, end card), sound effects, index.html.

Render: `npx hyperframes render -o out.mp4`
